# Presentation outline template

Fill this into slide content from `findings.json`. Output as structured prose
(one block per slide) that the client can drop into Google Slides, PowerPoint,
Marp, reveal.js, or a doc — this skill does not mandate a slide engine. Obey
every rule in `SKILL.md`, including **human names only — never an internal code**
(no `SEM-02`, no `adoption.reach`, not even in parentheses). Keep each slide to
what fits on a slide: a headline, a few bullets, and the numbers that matter.

**If `meta.framing == "light"`:** drop the stage/readiness framing on every slide.
Keep the findings, coverage, and recommendations slides, phrased as observed
facts. Replace "readiness by domain" with "what we observed, by domain."

### Slide 3..N — Readiness by domain (one slide per domain)

_For each entry in `domains[]`:_

- **Headline:** _[Domain id] — target [target_stage name], today [readiness name],
  gap [gap]._
- **Binding constraint:** _named (or inferred from `dimension_scores` if
  `binding_constraints` is empty and readiness < target) — the one thing to fix._
- **Confidence:** _`confidence`._
- **Not measured here:** _`unscored_dimensions`, named as caveats._

### Slide — What the scan found

- _2–4 bullets from the `findings` blocks present: metric-definition multiplicity,
  security exposure, unused capacity, governance posture. Human names, real
  numbers, respect `usage_measured`._

### Slide — What was and wasn't measured

- _Every `coverage[]` row not `"ok"`, plus unscored dimensions. Mandatory —
  never let a blind spot pass as a clean result._

### Slide — Recommendations roadmap

- _One item per binding constraint, ordered by the gate it blocks. Each framed as
  the demonstration test to pass, not a product to buy._
- _Mark any stage 4→5 or 5→6 crossing as a high-cost, high-risk step._
- _Where a constraint is unmeasured: "measure this first."_

### Slide — Before you start: capture a baseline

- _The two or three measures to record and date before remediating
  (`framework-brief.md`)._

### Slide — Closing / provenance

  does not modify the assessment. No overall maturity score is implied._
