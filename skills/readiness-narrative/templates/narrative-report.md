# Narrative report template

Fill this skeleton from `findings.json`. Instructional notes are in _italics_ —
replace them with real content and delete the note. Obey every rule in
`SKILL.md`: no composite score, coverage-first, grounded in the data, per-domain,
and **human names only — never an internal code** (no `SEM-02`, no
`adoption.reach`, not even in parentheses).

**If `meta.framing == "light"`:** omit every "stage", "readiness", "maturity", and
"target" reference. Keep the header, findings, coverage, and recommendations
sections, phrased as observed facts with no ladder language. Skip the
per-domain-readiness section's stage numbers and instead describe what was
observed and what to do.

coverage section._
